First release. Shared bill tracking for a household of two to five people,
running on your own hardware.

## What it does

- Enter a bill and split it evenly, by exact amounts, by percentage, or hand
  the whole thing to one person.
- Records who actually paid. Everyone else owes them, and the payer's own
  share is settled straight away — nobody owes themselves.
- Balances net both directions down to a single figure per pair. If one of you
  owes $60 and the other owes $25, you see one debt of $35, not two.
- Recurring bills — rent fortnightly, power quarterly — issued automatically.
  A server that was switched off catches up when it comes back rather than
  skipping.
- Bills whose amount changes each time arrive on schedule with no figure,
  waiting to be filled in. Guessing a power bill would be worse than asking.
- No-reply email when a bill is added, three days before it falls due, and
  weekly while it is overdue.
- Separate layouts for phone and desktop, chosen before the page is sent.
- CSV export of every bill and share, for a spreadsheet or tax time.

## What it does not do

It moves no money. There is no payment processing, no bank connection and no
card handling. It records what is owed and shows a PayID so people transfer it
themselves.

## Running it

One container and one SQLite file — no database to run alongside it, and the
whole thing backs up as a single file.

`SESSION_SECRET` is required. Set `APP_URL` to the address housemates actually
type, and `TZ` to your timezone. See `README.md`, or `docs/truenas.md` for a
step-by-step TrueNAS deployment.

## Under the surface

Three decisions that keep the numbers honest:

- Amounts are integer cents everywhere. Splitting uses the largest-remainder
  method, so shares always sum to the bill total rather than drifting a cent.
- Dates are stored as calendar dates rather than timestamps, so a bill due on
  the 1st never shows as the 31st to somebody in another timezone.
- Bills are voided rather than deleted, and departed housemates deactivated
  rather than removed, so the history stays readable.

196 tests cover the money, splitting, dates, recurrence, balances and email.
